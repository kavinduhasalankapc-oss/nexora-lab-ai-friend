Android microphone permission

Open android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml and add these lines above <application>:

<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO"/>
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET"/>

Speech recognition availability and Sinhala locale support depend on the Android device and installed speech services.
The starter app uses Android speech recognition and the phone's TTS engine. These may require network access and are NOT guaranteed to work offline.
